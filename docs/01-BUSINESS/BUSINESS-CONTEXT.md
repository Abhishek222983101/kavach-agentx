# Business / project context

> **What this is:** How the business or project domain actually works, in the owner's words. Every feature depends on this. For a personal project, treat "business" as the project's domain, users and constraints.
> **How to fill:** Ask one question at a time. Record the owner's words first, then a plain-language summary. Never invent numbers. Mark what is confirmed, observed, assumed, or unknown.
> **Truth labels:** `[CONFIRMED]` · `[OBSERVED]` · `[ASSUMED]` · `[UNKNOWN]` · `[DECISION]`
