# Deep context

> **What this is:** The "why" behind the work. Not the feature list — the business logic, history, ideology, unwritten rules, real examples, and contradictions.
> **How to fill:** One topic at a time. Ask for a real example from last week. Keep going until you can explain the rule to a new employee without guessing. **This is the most valuable file for the next person.**
> **Truth labels:** `[CONFIRMED]` · `[OBSERVED]` · `[ASSUMED]` · `[UNKNOWN]` · `[DECISION]`

## How to dig

Use this ladder for every topic. Stop when the answer stops changing.

1. **What** is done?
2. **Who** does it, and who is not allowed?
3. **When** is it triggered? What happens before and after?
4. **How** is it done today, step by step?
5. **Why** is it done this way? Is that a rule, a habit, or a workaround?
6. **What happens when it goes wrong?** Who fixes it? What does it cost?
7. **What is the exception** that happens once a month and breaks the normal flow?
8. **What used to happen?** What changed, and why?
9. **What would make you change it?**
10. **What did I misunderstand?** Read it back and ask for corrections.

Ask for **last Tuesday**, not "the process". Concrete examples expose the real rules.
